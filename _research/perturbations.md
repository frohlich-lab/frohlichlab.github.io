Our models need datasets at a scale and level of multiplexing beyond what conventional wet-lab workflows produce. We are building high-throughput, reproducible pipelines for single-cell perturbation proteomics, centred on mass cytometry: it resolves single cells, scales to thousands of conditions through barcoding, and offers established markers of cell cycle state, differentiation and signalling.
