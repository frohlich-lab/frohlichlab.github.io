Mechanistic models are usually built from literature curation, modelling dogma such as mass-action kinetics, and many subjective, rarely documented decisions. We aim to augment this process with data-driven methods, such as symbolic regression \[[de Pomereu & Fröhlich 2026]\] and universal differential equations \[[Persson *et al.* 2026a], [2026b][Persson *et al.* 2026b]\], and with agentic approaches that combine them with existing mechanistic knowledge \[[de Pomereu *et al.* 2026]\].
